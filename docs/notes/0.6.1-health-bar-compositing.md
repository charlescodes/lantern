# Lantern 0.6.1 Health-Bar Compositing

Lantern 0.6.1 is a presentation-only patch for the Three.js actor health bars introduced in 0.6.0. It does not change gameplay, snapshots, recordings, scenarios, Fireball definitions, performance reports, or replay profiles.

## Symptom

In the Three.js renderer, a visible actor's health fill could look black even though its instance color was green, amber, or red. The intended color became briefly visible while TrueSight faded the bar into or out of concealment. Canvas2D was unaffected.

## Cause and repair

The dark track used a transparent material while the colored fill used an opaque material. Three.js collected them into separate render queues, so the opaque fill rendered before the transparent track regardless of the meshes' relative render order. The dark track then composited over the fill.

Both materials now use the transparent queue. Depth testing and writing remain disabled, and the resident fill mesh retains a higher render order than the resident track mesh. TrueSight still drives the same shared opacity and mask nodes, so the whole bar conceals with its actor without changing simulation visibility.

## Regression boundary

The focused Three.js presentation test asserts the shared transparent-queue classification, disabled depth state, and track-before-fill ordering in addition to existing instance capacity, color, scale, placement, TrueSight, and resource-identity checks. Human acceptance should confirm green, amber, and red fills in normal visibility and throughout reveal/conceal transitions on both automatic 3D and forced WebGL 2 routes.
