# Lantern 0.9.2 Elevator Spell Impacts

> **Status:** current application checkpoint
>
> **Application/package:** `0.9.2` · **Snapshot/recording schema:** v13 · **Authoring map:** v5

Lantern 0.9.2 makes elevator geometry a first-class Fireball blocker without
changing elevator movement, rider support, or dynamic-body collision.

## Current behavior

- A Fireball on a lower elevator layer explodes on the square deck while the
  lift dwells at its lower stop, and on the circular piston/shaft while the
  lift travels or dwells at its upper stop.
- A Fireball on the upper layer explodes on the square deck only while it is
  dwelling at the upper stop. It does not collide with a piston below that
  floor, avoiding an invisible upper-floor blocker.
- The impact event records `hit.kind: "elevator"`, the runtime elevator ID, and
  the stable connector ID. The normal explosion, particles, sound, and blast
  response paths continue from that impact.

## Replay boundary

Schema v13 records the required
`elevatorProjectileCollisionProfile: "elevator-projectile-collision-v1"`.
Schema-v12 recordings continue to load their authoring-map projection but use
the frozen no-elevator-projectile-collision behavior. Older schemas retain
their existing compatibility rules.
