# Lantern 0.7.0 Tactical Wizard AI

## Contract

Lantern 0.7.0 replaces the live `basic-wizard-v1` encounter behavior with `tactical-wizard-v1`. It advances application/package/UI and snapshot/recording schema to v7 while leaving scenario v3, map v1, Fireball definition v1, performance-report v2, combat damage, encounter cadence, actor physics, and presentation topology unchanged.

The four-entry enemy typed-array pool remains the authority for stable identity, health, velocity channels, cooldowns, successful-cast sequences, and AI state. Its swap removal now also moves navigation, movement-goal, strafe, aim, threat, dodge, and retreat components as one identity.

## Shared navigation field

All enemies consume one simulation-authoritative distance field rooted at the player's cell. Dijkstra expansion uses integer cost `10` for cardinal neighbors and `14` for diagonals. Neighbor order is north, east, south, west, northeast, southeast, southwest, northwest. A diagonal is traversable only when its destination and both adjoining cardinal cells are floor, preventing corner cutting.

The completed and building cost arrays, binary-heap queue, queue positions, and tie sequences are preallocated for the current map dimensions. At most 2,048 cells are expanded in one fixed tick. A rebuild starts when the player changes cells or an accepted tile/load command changes the map revision. The previous completed array remains readable while the replacement is building. If no field has completed, movement falls back to the 0.6 direct range-correction behavior; an unreachable or locally exhausted gradient also uses that bounded direct fallback.

Approach follows strictly decreasing finite cost. Range withdrawal and low-health retreat follow strictly increasing finite cost. Equal gradient choices retain the fixed neighbor order. Rocks and actors are intentionally absent from the field and continue through existing local dynamic collision, so they may deflect or temporarily block an otherwise valid route.

## Engagement and deterministic strafe

The `6-9m` engagement band remains authoritative. Beyond `9m` the wizard approaches; inside `6m` it withdraws; inside the band it moves tangentially at `3.5m/s`. Initial left/right direction and each `90-180` tick interval come from named hashes of simulation seed, stable enemy spawn sequence, and enemy-local decision sequence. The side flips at every scheduled change.

These decisions never consume global simulation RNG. Adding another enemy, changing particle count or palette variation, or making unrelated player casts cannot shift an existing enemy's strafe schedule.

## Intercept aim and shared spell updates

Every tactical aim update solves the smallest positive constant-velocity interception time from current authoritative player position/velocity and the currently applied Fireball projectile speed. No positive solution falls back to direct aim. A valid solution is clamped to `min(projectile lifetime, 1.5s)`, then 75% of that lead is applied to the target point.

Current-player grid line-of-sight remains the cast gate. A blocked or cooling shot stays ready. Apply actions still precede autonomous casts in a tick, so a newly applied speed affects that tick's enemy intercept immediately. Enemy cooldowns, cast sequences, and effect seeds remain caster-local, and existing effects retain captured revisions.

## Hostile-projectile dodge

A player-team Fireball is a threat only when all of these are true:

- closest approach is from `0.25s` through `0.90s` ahead and within remaining projectile lifetime;
- current center distance is at least `2m`;
- predicted miss distance is no greater than enemy radius plus projectile radius plus `0.20m`.

Simultaneous threats choose the earliest approach, then the smaller miss, effect ID, and projectile ID. The wizard evaluates both directions perpendicular to projectile travel across the full 18-tick map-clear dodge path. Among legal sides it chooses the greater predicted closest-approach clearance; an exact tie uses the enemy-local `dodge-tie` hash lane.

Dodge locomotion is immediately `6m/s` for 18 fixed ticks. It may temporarily override approach, strafe, withdrawal, or retreat movement without changing retreat status. When the dodge ends, 105 complete cooldown ticks must pass before a new dodge can begin.

## Retreat and regeneration

At `30` health or below, the wizard enters retreat, stops autonomous casting, and follows increasing navigation cost. Normal damage resets the existing five-second regeneration delay; regeneration remains `1 HP/s`. Dodge can interrupt the retreat vector but cannot clear the retreat flag. The wizard re-engages only when health is at least `60` at a decision tick.

## Schema, replay, and diagnostics

Schema-v7 recording configuration stores:

```json
{
  "gameplayProfile": "obelisk-duel-v1",
  "enemyAiProfile": "tactical-wizard-v1"
}
```

Schema-v6 recordings accept only the frozen obelisk/basic pairing (or their historical pre-combat pairing) and reproduce direct aim without tactical state affecting behavior. Schema-v2 through v5 retain their earlier frozen boundaries. Tactical decisions remain autonomous simulation rather than synthetic commands.

Snapshots and inspector records expose global field build/version/revision state plus each enemy's behavior state, movement goal, field cost/version, strafe side/change timer, predicted aim and lead time, tracked threat effect/projectile, dodge timers/direction, and retreat flag. `window.__lantern.enemyDiagnostics(id?)` returns the same read-only state for every enemy or one stable ID. No AI editor, mutation command, or Spell Lab AI field is added.

## Presentation and validation

Canvas2D and Three.js consume the extended snapshot without new meshes, materials, buffers, lights, art, or warmup topology. TrueSight concealment remains presentation-only and cannot perturb navigation, aim, dodge, retreat, hashes, or replay.

Automated acceptance covers corridor/doorway reachability, disconnected regions, diagonal corner prevention, fixed ties, bounded incremental work, stale-field use, map invalidation, intercept cases and clamping, immediate spell-speed updates, LOS gating, genuine and rejected threats, legal dodge sides/cooldown/simultaneous selection, strafe schedules/range correction, retreat/casting suppression/recovery, pool compaction, deterministic independence, exact schema-v7 replay, frozen schema-v6 direct aim, and all legacy replay fixtures.
