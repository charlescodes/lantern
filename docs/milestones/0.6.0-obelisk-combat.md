# Lantern 0.6.0 Obelisk Combat Foundation

## Contract

Lantern 0.6.0 adds one replay-safe combat encounter to the default debug arena without adding stages or changing the Fireball authoring format. A scenario-authored, invulnerable obelisk stands at world position `(20.5, 18.5)` on solid cell `(20, 18)`. It behaves exactly like a wall for actor motion, projectiles, blast line-of-effect, AI line-of-sight, and TrueSight, but both renderers replace that wall cell with procedural obelisk geometry.

Legacy scenario v2 and map v1 documents remain valid sandbox arenas. Because they contain no obelisk, they spawn no enemies. Scenario v3 permits existing rocks plus at most one `{ "kind": "obelisk", "x", "z" }` entity. The obelisk must be cell-centered, occupy an in-bounds solid cell, and avoid player or rock overlap. Its entity cannot be removed and its supporting cell cannot be erased in the editor.

This release explicitly adds no stage counter, end portal, obelisk damage or scaling, loot, mana, loadouts, sound, contact damage, player customization, enemy-specific spell definitions, networking, or multiplayer authority.

## Encounter and basic wizard

The encounter attempts its first spawn on fixed tick 1 and another every 1,800 ticks, or 30 seconds. It holds at most four living enemies. Capped or obstructed attempts are counted and skipped without retries or queued debt. Every attempt advances through the eight adjacent cell centers in fixed order: north, east, south, west, northeast, southeast, southwest, northwest.

Each enemy has a stable pool ID and stable spawn sequence. The four-entry typed-array pool uses swap removal, copying identity, position history, locomotion/external velocity channels, physical values, health/regeneration state, cooldown, cast sequence, ready tick, line-of-sight, and AI state together.

`basic-wizard-v1` always knows the player's current simulation position. It approaches beyond `9m`, withdraws inside `6m`, and holds between `6m` and `9m`. It uses the same acceleration, braking, wall collision, wall sliding, and dynamic-body collision model as the player. It attempts a direct shot every 75 ticks (1.25 seconds) and also observes the currently applied Fireball cooldown. A blocked shot remains ready until line-of-sight and cooldown both permit it.

The basic profile intentionally does not strafe, lead targets, dodge, pathfind, or retreat to regenerate. Rocks and actors are handled by local collision response only, so walls can trap or delay a wizard. TrueSight does not contribute knowledge to this behavior.

## Shared spell and combat rules

Player and enemy wizards cast through the same global Fireball registry. Apply actions execute before casts in a tick and affect future casts from either team immediately. Every projectile, impact, particle, palette sample, and light lease retains its captured definition revision after Apply or caster death. Enemy casts do not advance the player's cooldown, successful-cast sequence, or Spell Lab preview seed.

Player seed derivation remains unchanged. An enemy effect seed is derived from simulation seed, stable enemy spawn sequence, spell code, and that enemy's successful-cast sequence. Both paths are independent of global simulation RNG and one another.

Player and enemy wizards share a `0.3m` radius, `75kg` mass, player movement values, `100` maximum health, and `1 HP/s` regeneration after 300 damage-free ticks. Health damage is a symmetric non-authorable combat rule:

```text
direct opponent hit = 25
splash damage = 25 × clamp(1 - surfaceDistance / capturedBlastRadius, 0, 1)
```

Solid cells, including the obelisk, block splash damage. Casters and same-team actors are immune to health damage and ignored by same-team projectile collision. The existing physical explosion impulse remains neutral and can still move casters, allies, opponents, and rocks. Projectiles fired by a dead enemy stay active; dead enemies compact only after all projectile processing for that tick.

## Defeat and reset

Zero player health enters `defeated` for 90 fixed ticks (1.5 seconds). Autonomous movement, casting, enemies, projectiles, particles, regeneration, and encounter cadence freeze. Movement and cast commands are ignored, while explicit reset, Apply, and Clear actions remain accepted. The UI reports `DEFEATED`, the remaining restart time, and a non-modal overlay so Spell Lab remains accessible.

When the countdown expires, reset uses the same simulation seed and restores full health, authored rocks, encounter cadence, empty enemy/effect pools, deterministic IDs/sequences, and an empty command-log baseline. It preserves the currently applied Fireball revision; the browser-owned Spell Lab draft also remains intact.

## Snapshots, replay, and diagnostics

Application/package/UI version is `0.6.0`. Snapshot and command-recording schema is v6; scenario schema is v3. Map v1, Fireball definition v1, and performance-report v2 do not change.

Schema-v6 snapshots add gameplay and AI profiles, level/defeat state, encounter timing and skips, obelisks, enemies, player/enemy health and regeneration, projectile owner kind/team, structured player/enemy explosion ownership and hits, bounded combat events, and enemy-pool telemetry. `queryAt`, pinning, and inspector records recognize `enemyWizard` and `obelisk`.

Schema-v6 recording configuration stores:

```json
{
  "gameplayProfile": "obelisk-duel-v1",
  "enemyAiProfile": "basic-wizard-v1"
}
```

Enemy decisions remain deterministic autonomous simulation and are not written as input commands. Schema-v5 recordings keep versioned Fireball behavior but select frozen pre-combat gameplay. Schema-v2 through v4 continue through their genuine historical profiles. The browser probe `window.__lantern.encounterDiagnostics()` returns level state, restart timing, spawn cadence/skips, enemy identities, health, cooldowns, cast sequences, and recent combat events.

## Presentation

Canvas2D and Three.js render the same hostile silhouette in a distinct color and the same procedural obelisk without external art or new lights. Every living actor has a screen-right `0.10m × 0.90m` vertical health track filled from the bottom: green above 50%, amber from 25% through 50%, and red at or below 25%.

The Three.js adapter creates all four enemy instances and five health-track/fill instances before asynchronous renderer warmup. Their geometries, materials, instance buffers, and identities remain resident. Fireball point-light capacity and lease topology are unchanged.

TrueSight conceals each hidden enemy mesh and health bar. It also conceals every hostile projectile, particle, emissive response, and light through the existing shared effect masking path. Concealment is presentation-only and cannot alter collision, casting, damage, AI, recordings, or replay.

## Validation

Automated acceptance covers scenario-v3 validation and legacy imports; protected cells; deterministic spawn cadence, cap, skips, and reset; actor/grid/rock collision; pool-swap identity; independent caster cooldowns, sequences, and seeds; same-tick Apply ordering; captured revisions after death; direct/splash damage, falloff, occlusion, immunity, pass-through, neutral impulse, regeneration, enemy death, and defeat timing; exact schema-v6 replay and genuine schema-v2/v3/v4/v5 fixtures; Canvas2D/Three.js health and obelisk parity; TrueSight-ready resident materials; bounded event history; and maximum-definition four-enemy combat soak under the `8ms` simulation p99 target.
