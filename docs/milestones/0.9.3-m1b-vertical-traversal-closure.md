# Lantern 0.9.3 M1B Vertical Traversal Closure

> **Status:** frozen application checkpoint
>
> **Application/package:** `0.9.3` · **Snapshot/recording schema:** v14 · **Authoring map:** v5

Lantern 0.9.3 formally closes M1B.1–M1B.4. It records the completed vertical
traversal foundation as one application checkpoint without changing the
authoring-map format or creating another recording-schema version.

## Closed behavior

- Eligible bodies own limited world Y, gravity, support state, and an
  independent runtime layer while X/Z collision remains authoritative.
- Autonomous two-stop elevators are timer-driven, unstoppable kinematic
  supports. Riders retain ordinary X/Z movement; payloads do not select,
  slow, or reverse a lift.
- Single-cell apertures enforce full-footprint fit and positive clearance.
  Holes, upper elevator shafts, multi-floor falling, intermediate landings,
  rim attraction, and falling air control use the shared vertical pipeline.
- Committed player jumps clear fitting holes and explicitly airborne-passable
  clutter, then land through the normal support path.
- Grounded pressure plates and breakaway surfaces are bounded authored
  mechanisms. A breakaway uses its 18-tick countdown before installing the
  ordinary square-hole aperture; it is not a scripted teleport or a general
  trigger graph.
- Fireballs hit elevator decks and applicable lower-floor shafts under the
  existing layer-local collision rules.
- Live editor connector placement and the active authored floor remain
  independent from runtime body layers. Reset and Restore positions rebuild
  authored starts rather than persisting runtime movement.

The detailed current implementation contract is
[M1B.1–M1B.4 vertical bodies, elevator, holes, jumping, plates, and
breakaways](../notes/generic-vertical-bodies-and-elevator.md).

## Compatibility boundary

Schema v14 records `breakaway-floor-v1`. Schemas v2–v13 retain their frozen
behavior, including schema v13's `elevator-projectile-collision-v1` and schema
v12's committed-jump edge. Authoring-map v5 remains the current source format;
no map migration belongs to this application-only release.

  `test/breakaway_floor.test.js`, `test/elevator_authoring_runtime.test.js`,

- The manual bottom-to-top-to-bottom M1B route is recorded in the

## Deliberate non-goals

M1B does not add general 3D stacking/tipping/crushing, multi-stop elevators,
cross-floor AI routing or perception, a generic trigger graph, doors, or
networking. M1C begins narrowly with authored navigation topology.
